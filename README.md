# Credit_Card_Dashboard
This Power BI dashboard provides an in-depth analysis of credit card transaction data, offering insights into revenue trends, customer behaviors, and performance metrics across various dimensions. Below is an overview of the key sections of the dashboard and what they represent.
<br/>
Interactive view can be found on [https://aymang.streamlit.app](https://aymang.streamlit.app/Credit_Card_Dashboard) </br>

  <h2>1. Key Performance Metrics</h2>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Total Revenue:</strong> $56.52M</li>
        <li><strong>Total Transactions:</strong> 656K</li>
        <li><strong>Total Transaction Amount:</strong> $45.53M</li>
        <li><strong>Total Interest Earned:</strong> $7.98M</li>
    </ul>

  <h2>2. Quarterly Revenue and Transaction Trends</h2>
    <ul>
        <li>Visualizes revenue and transaction counts across Q1 to Q4.</li>
        <li>Helps identify seasonal trends and peak transaction periods.</li>
    </ul>

  <h2>3. Revenue by Expenditure Type</h2>
    <ul>
        <li>Shows revenue distribution across categories like bills, entertainment, fuel, grocery, and food.</li>
        <li>Top categories include <strong>Bills ($14M)</strong> and <strong>Entertainment ($10M)</strong>.</li>
    </ul>

<h2>4. Revenue by Customer Demographics</h2>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>By State:</strong> Top states include TX, NY, and CA, each contributing approximately $13M in revenue.</li>
        <li><strong>By Job:</strong> Businessmen contribute the highest revenue ($17.7M), followed by white-collar workers ($10.2M).</li>
        <li><strong>By Age Group:</strong> The 60+ age group generates the most revenue ($14M).</li>
        <li><strong>By Income Group:</strong> High-income customers contribute $23M in revenue.</li>
    </ul>

<h2>5. Revenue by Card Type</h2>
    <ul>
        <li>Breaks down revenue by card categories: Blue, Silver, Gold, and Platinum.</li>
        <li><strong>Blue Cards:</strong> Generate the majority of revenue ($47M).</li>
    </ul>

<h2>6. Revenue by Chip Usage</h2>
    <ul>
        <li>Analyzes how transactions are made: swipe, chip, or online.</li>
        <li><strong>Swipe Transactions:</strong> Account for $36M of the total revenue.</li>
    </ul>

<h2>7. Education and Dependency Insights</h2>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Education Level:</strong> Graduates contribute the highest revenue ($13M).</li>
        <li><strong>Dependents:</strong> Customers with two dependents contribute $8.9M in revenue.</li>
    </ul>

<h2>8. Weekly Revenue Trends</h2>
    <ul>
        <li>Shows weekly revenue patterns to identify regular fluctuations or spikes in transactions.</li>
    </ul>

<h2>9. Summary Table</h2>
    <ul>
        <li>Provides aggregated data for card types, total revenue, transaction amounts, and interest earned.</li>
        <li>Highlights major contributors and overall performance metrics for each card category.</li>
    </ul>

<p>
        This dashboard serves as a comprehensive tool for analyzing credit card transactions and understanding customer behavior. By leveraging these insights, stakeholders can make informed decisions to enhance financial strategies and customer satisfaction.
</p>
